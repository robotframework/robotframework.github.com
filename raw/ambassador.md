**From first-timers to fast-trackers — you help others get started.**

Ambassadors support students, answer

questions, and mentor newcomers who are just discovering Robot Framework.

### Share Knowledge Publicly

**Talk, write, and show — your voice scales!**

As an ambassador, you play a key role in spreading knowledge and inspiring others by helping them understand what Robot Framework is capable of.

Whether you're presenting at events, leading workshops, writing useful content, or creating media, your contributions make the community stronger and more informed.

#### Build the Tools

**Robot Framework is only as strong as its ecosystem — and you help build it.**

Ambassadors contribute to the technical side by developing, maintaining, and supporting tools that others depend on.

- Create or maintain Robot Framework libraries and tools
- Contribute to the core or help fix issues
- Write documentation and practical examples for tools
- Answer user questions related to specific libraries

</page-card>

<page-card max-width="40" max-width-mobile="90" styling="default">

#### Promote Learning

**Training matters — and you make it accessible.**

Ambassadors support formal learning and certification so users can confidently grow their skills and credibility with Robot Framework.

- Advocate for training and certification in your organization or community
- Help others prepare for certification
- Encourage your company to become a training or certification partner
- Contribute to the development of certification programs

</page-card>

<page-card max-width="40" max-width-mobile="90" styling="default">

#### **Open new doors**

Ambassadors help expand Robot Framework into new industries, companies, and domains. You find places where it fits, and help others see the value.

- Introduce Robot Framework to new industries or market segments
- Help companies adopt Robot Framework for the first time
- Act as a connector between organizations and community resources
- Mentor professionals exploring Robot Framework in new contexts

<page-section title="Want in? Here's how it works" width="default">

The program runs in rolling quarters. You apply anytime, we review applications every quarter.

1. **Apply**
Fill in [the application form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfcdKpl8Fb4x6o9aN08dKPhONc2Gtqtnnc5Grbh53xJEvJPAg/viewform?usp=sharing&ouid=116836475553179546044) with your interests and skills.
2. **Review**
We evaluate your background and motivation during the following quarter.
3. **Alignment Call**
If selected, you'll have a quick chat with us to set expectations and meet your ambassador buddy.
4. **Candidate Period**
You complete two self-chosen activities within a quarter—this shows commitment.
5. **Welcome Onboard**Once complete, you're officially a Robot Framework Ambassador:

  1. You'll be listed on the website
  2. Get RF swag
  3. Join the ambassador Slack
  4. Eligible for rewards

### Quarterly Cycle Example

Apply in Q1 → Review in Q2 → Contribute in Q3 → Official Ambassador in Q4

</page-section>

**What we expect:**

- Stay active and contribute regularly
- Represent Robot Framework positively and professionally
- Join quarterly meetings, log your activities
- Share your work—help others grow

**What you get:**

- Your own profile on the website
- Swag (including a custom shirt)
- Access to our ambassador Slack
- A peer buddy for support
