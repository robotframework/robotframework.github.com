# Robot Framework Foundation

> A non-profit consortium of companies funding the development of Robot Framework, its infrastructure and its community events. Membership is open — here is what it costs and how to join.

<hero-banner :center-title="true" eyebrow="More Than A Project" title="<span class="ink-teal">Robot Framework</span><br>Foundation<br><span class="rf-mark rf-mark--full" ></span>" title-size="medium" variant="small">

> **Robot Framework**® **Foundation** is a non-profit consortium that fosters the growth of Robot Framework.
> 
> It was founded by companies with a common interest to ensure the development of Robot Framework now and in the future.
> 
> We are always looking for new members to join us.

</hero-banner>

<page-section title="What We Do" width="narrow">

Robot Framework is free and open source, and it stays that way. The Foundation is
here to make sure it also stays **well maintained** — paid for by the organisations
that rely on it, for everyone who uses it.

<item-row :same-height="true" list-type="wrapping">
<page-card max-width="50" max-width-mobile="100" gap="0.33">

#### We pay the maintainers

Developing, releasing and supporting Robot Framework is real work. We fund the
people who do it, so the project never has to depend on spare evenings.

</page-card>

<page-card max-width="50" max-width-mobile="100" gap="0.33">

#### We back the wider ecosystem

Robot Framework is more than its core. We can sponsor other projects around it,
so the tools and libraries you build on keep moving too.

</page-card>

<page-card max-width="50" max-width-mobile="100" gap="0.33">

#### We keep the lights on

The public websites, documentation and the community platforms everyone relies on —
we run them and we pay for them.

</page-card>

<page-card max-width="50" max-width-mobile="100" gap="0.33">

#### We bring people together

Meetups and the yearly conference, [RoboCon](https://robocon.io/) — where the
community learns from each other and meets the people behind the project.

</page-card>
</item-row>

Every euro goes back into the tools and the ecosystem; administration is kept to a
minimum. <br />


And we fund the work — what goes into Robot Framework
is still decided in the open, by the community.

[Read more about the Foundation, unfolded](https://forum.robotframework.org/t/robot-framework-foundation-unfolded/276)

</page-section>

### When your company becomes a member, you will…

<item-row :same-height="true" list-type="wrapping">
<page-card max-width="50" max-width-mobile="100" gap="0.33">

#### Ensure the continuity of the Robot Framework Project

Your membership fee funds the maintainers who keep Robot Framework developed, released and supported.

</page-card>

<page-card max-width="50" max-width-mobile="100" gap="0.33">

#### Get visibility

Your company can be listed publicly as a member, if you wish.

</page-card>

<page-card max-width="50" max-width-mobile="100" gap="0.33">

#### Discounted tickets for official events

- Discounted tickets to RoboCon
- 20% discount on the certification exam for the official Robot Framework certificate, [RFCP](https://cert.robotframework.org/)

</page-card>

<page-card max-width="50" max-width-mobile="100" gap="0.33">

#### Become a certified training provider

Robot Framework's official certificate has accredited companies that offer training for the exam. As a Foundation member, your company is welcome to apply.

</page-card>
</item-row>

<page-section title="How To Join" width="narrow">

If you would like to become a member of the Robot Framework Foundation:

1. Fill in [the application](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScOilsVlkYEB9a48OBmFG7NFTstSs5H5rqPEHjruC-gbE-NaQ/viewform)
2. The Foundation board votes on it according to our rules
3. Once accepted, you are added to the website and the mailing list
4. Welcome — let's make the world of open source and automation better

Questions about membership? Email [board@robotframework.org](mailto:board@robotframework.org), or book a meeting with the Executive Director below, and we will get back to you.
