More Than A Project

Robot Framework
Foundation

Robot Framework® Foundation is a non-profit consortium that fosters the growth of Robot Framework.

It was founded by companies with a common interest to ensure the development of Robot Framework now and in the future.

We are always looking for new members to join us.

What We Do

Robot Framework is free and open source, and it stays that way. The Foundation is here to make sure it also stays well maintained — paid for by the organisations that rely on it, for everyone who uses it.

We pay the maintainers

Developing, releasing and supporting Robot Framework is real work. We fund the people who do it, so the project never has to depend on spare evenings.

We back the wider ecosystem

Robot Framework is more than its core. We can sponsor other projects around it, so the tools and libraries you build on keep moving too.

We keep the lights on

The public websites, documentation and the community platforms everyone relies on — we run them and we pay for them.

We bring people together

Meetups and the yearly conference, RoboCon — where the community learns from each other and meets the people behind the project.

Every euro goes back into the tools and the ecosystem; administration is kept to a minimum.
And we fund the work — what goes into Robot Framework is still decided in the open, by the community.

How To Join

If you would like to become a member of the Robot Framework Foundation:

  1. Fill in the application
  2. The Foundation board votes on it according to our rules
  3. Once accepted, you are added to the website and the mailing list
  4. Welcome — let's make the world of open source and automation better

Questions about membership? Email board@robotframework.org and we will get back to you.

For existing members

If your contact person changes, or for other membership matters, please contact admin@robotframework.org.
